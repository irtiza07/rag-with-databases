# Remote Work Policy

Brewly uses a hybrid work model for office-based roles such as marketing, finance, engineering, and customer support. Roastery, café, and warehouse roles are on-site by nature and are not covered by this policy.

Office-based employees are expected in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, which are our "anchor days" for team meetings and collaboration. The other three weekdays can be worked from home or from the office, whichever you prefer.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Pacific Time. Outside of those hours you may structure your day however works best for you, as long as your manager agrees.

Employees may work fully remotely from another location for up to 4 weeks per calendar year. Requests must be submitted to your manager at least two weeks in advance. Working from outside the United States requires additional approval from People Ops for tax reasons.

Brewly provides a one-time $500 home office stipend to new office-based employees, which can be used for a desk, chair, monitor, or other equipment.

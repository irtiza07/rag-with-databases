# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Brewly reimburses reasonable business expenses that employees pay for out of pocket. All expenses must be submitted through the Expensify app within 30 days of the purchase, with a photo of the receipt attached.

Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at approved client dinners.

Flights must be booked in economy class through our travel portal at least 14 days before departure, unless the trip is urgent. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $250 per night.

Any single expense over $500 requires written approval from your manager before the purchase is made.

Learning and development is a priority at Brewly: every employee has a $1,200 yearly education budget for courses, books, conferences, and barista certifications.

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle, which runs on the 15th and the last day of each month.

# Brewly Coffee Co. — Company Overview

Brewly Coffee Co. is a specialty coffee roaster founded in 2017 in Portland, Oregon, by Maya Chen and Luis Ortega. We source beans directly from small farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Indonesia, and roast them in small batches at our Portland roastery.

Our mission is simple: make exceptional coffee accessible while paying farmers fairly. Every bag we sell carries a "Direct Trade" label, which means we pay at least 25% above the Fairtrade minimum price.

Today Brewly has 140 employees across three locations: the Portland roastery and headquarters, a café and training center in Seattle, and a distribution warehouse in Sacramento. We sell through our online store, 12 company-owned cafés, and more than 300 wholesale partners.

Our core values are Curiosity, Craft, and Care. Curiosity means we keep learning and experimenting. Craft means we sweat the details. Care means we look after our customers, our farmers, and each other.

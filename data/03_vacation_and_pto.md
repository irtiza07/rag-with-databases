# Vacation and Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year. PTO accrues gradually each pay period, at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours.

After 3 years of service, the yearly PTO allowance increases to 25 days. After 6 years, it increases to 30 days.

Up to 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Any additional unused days are forfeited on December 31st, so please plan your time off!

PTO requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. For shorter absences, one week of notice is enough.

Brewly also observes 10 paid company holidays, plus a company-wide "Harvest Break" during the last week of December when all offices and the roastery close. Cafés remain open with rotating volunteer staff who receive double pay.

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Every employee gets 10 paid sick days per year, and no doctor's note is required for absences of 3 days or less.

# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

Welcome to Brewly! Your first two weeks follow this checklist.

Day 1: Pick up your laptop and badge from IT at the Portland front desk (or have them shipped if you are remote). Set up your Google Workspace account, Slack, and the HR portal. Have a welcome coffee with your manager.

Week 1: Complete the security awareness training and the food safety course in the learning portal. Meet your onboarding buddy, a teammate who will answer questions during your first 90 days. Attend the "Seed to Cup" session, where our head roaster walks new hires through how coffee goes from farm to cup.

Week 2: Spend half a day shadowing at a café and half a day at the roastery, no matter what your role is. Set your 30-60-90 day goals with your manager.

Every new hire also receives a free bag of coffee every week for their first year, and a 50% discount on all Brewly products for as long as they work here.

Questions about onboarding can be sent to the #people-ops Slack channel or to peopleops@brewly.example.
